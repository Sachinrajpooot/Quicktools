How to edit a PDF online

  1. Choose the tool that matches your task. For example, use Merge PDF to combine files or Add Text to place text on pages.
  2. Read the tool's instructions and check its file and page limits before selecting a document.
  3. Choose your PDF from your phone, tablet or computer and set the available options.
  4. Run the operation, download the new file and open it to check the pages you changed.

Keep an unchanged copy of important documents. Editing, flattening, converting or rasterising a PDF can change its structure and may affect searchable text, interactive fields, links or digital signatures.

File handling, limits and document safety

QuickTools24 PDF operations are designed to process selected files in your browser. The site does not intentionally upload document contents to its own file-processing server. Your browser and device memory affect processing speed; third-party page resources, analytics and advertising providers may receive normal connection or usage information.

Limits depend on the operation. PDF files are limited to 25 MB each, PDF merging supports up to 60 MB combined, and raster-based operations such as compression or page rendering are limited to 60 selected pages per run. Check the individual tool page for its current limits and supported options.

The PDF compressor converts pages to images. It is not a lossless optimiser and can remove selectable text, links, form fields or vector detail. Avoid raster compression for signed, searchable, accessible, legal or archival documents unless you have checked the requirements and kept the original.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF editor free?

QuickTools24 PDF tools are available online without creating an account. Each tool page explains its current operation and limits.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

PDF operations are designed to run in your browser. QuickTools24 does not intentionally upload selected document contents to its own file-processing server.

Can I use the tools on a phone?

Yes. Open the tool in a modern mobile browser. Large PDFs may take longer or be limited by available device memory.

Will editing preserve a digital signature?

Do not assume it will. Modifying or converting a digitally signed PDF can invalidate its signature. Keep the original signed file and use an approved workflow for official documents.
