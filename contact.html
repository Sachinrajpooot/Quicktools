How to report a tool problem

  1. Tell us the exact name of the tool and the page address you were using.
  2. Describe the steps you followed and what you expected to happen.
  3. Include the error message, if one appeared.
  4. Mention your device and browser, for example Android with Chrome or a desktop browser.

Please do not email passwords, one-time codes, payment details or confidential documents. If a problem involves a private file, describe the issue without sharing the file unless it is absolutely necessary and safe to do so.

Suggestions and corrections

Suggestions about new tools, improvements to existing features, incorrect page information, broken links or website usability are welcome. If you are reporting an incorrect page, include its URL and the correction you recommend.

Privacy-related enquiries

For questions about information handling, cookies, analytics or advertising, contact us by email with “Privacy enquiry” in the subject line. You can also read our Privacy Policy.

Response time

Messages are reviewed as time permits. We cannot guarantee an individual response or a fixed response time, but clear details help us investigate reports more effectively.
