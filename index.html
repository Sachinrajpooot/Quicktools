ABOUT THE CREATOR

About QuickTools24

QuickTools24 is an independent online utility platform created to make everyday PDF, image, text, conversion and calculation tasks simple and accessible. The goal is to provide easy-to-use tools that work on mobile phones and computers, with a clear workflow and no unnecessary steps.

Founder & Creator

Platform specifications

  • Online PDF utilities for merging, splitting, compressing, converting and editing documents.
  • Image utilities for resizing, cropping, compressing and format conversion.
  • Text utilities for counting, cleaning and formatting text.
  • Everyday calculators and unit converters designed for quick results.
  • Responsive interface for mobile and desktop browsers.
  • Where supported, file processing takes place locally in the user's browser.

How files are processed

The PDF and image tools on this version are designed to process selected files locally in your browser. The website code does not intentionally upload the contents of your selected documents to a QuickTools24 server. Keep in mind that the page loads libraries and styling from third-party content delivery networks, which may receive normal connection information such as your IP address.

If you contact us, your email application opens with a message addressed to our support email. Sending that message is handled by your email provider, not stored by this website.

These services may receive technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, page requests, cookie identifiers and usage or ad-interaction data under their own policies.
