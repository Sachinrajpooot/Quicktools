How to Compress PDF Online for Free

PDF files can become difficult to email, upload or store when they contain high-resolution photos, scanned pages or many graphics. Compressing a PDF can make it smaller and easier to share, but the best result depends on what is inside the document and how much quality you need to preserve.

This guide explains how to reduce PDF file size, what to check before choosing a compression level and how to avoid relying on a file-size target that may not be achievable for every document.

What makes a PDF file large?

  • High-resolution images: Photos and scanned pages can account for much of a PDF's size.
  • Many pages: Long reports and document bundles naturally contain more data.
  • Embedded fonts and graphics: Some files include font data, vector artwork and other resources.
  • Scanned documents: A scan saved as a page-sized image may be larger than a text-based PDF.

How to reduce PDF size without making it unreadable

Keep an original copy before compressing an important document. Start with a moderate option if one is available, then compare the compressed file with the original. Pay particular attention to small text, tables, barcodes, seals, photographs and signatures. If the document is intended for an official form, follow that form's exact size, format and readability requirements.

Do not assume that every PDF can be reduced to a fixed target such as 200 KB. A short text-only PDF may become very small, while a long document containing detailed scans may remain larger even after compression.

What if the PDF is still too large?

Check whether the receiving website publishes a maximum file size. If it does, use that limit as your target and try a suitable compression setting. You can also split a large document into separate parts with the PDF Splitter, if the receiving service accepts multiple files. Avoid removing pages or reducing image quality unless the document's purpose allows it.

Can you compress a PDF on Android or iPhone?

A browser-based PDF tool can be used on many mobile devices without installing a separate app. Open the tool in your mobile browser, choose the file from the device picker and download the processed result. Very large PDFs may need more memory or processing time, so use a device with enough free space and keep the browser open while processing.

Privacy and document safety

Before using any online file service, read its privacy information and understand how files are processed and retained. Avoid using public or shared devices for confidential records. Keep a secure original copy and do not upload documents unless you are authorised to do so.

Frequently asked questions

Does compressing a PDF always reduce its size?

Not always by a meaningful amount. Some PDFs are already optimised or contain mostly text and vector data, so there may be little room to reduce their size.

Will PDF compression affect signatures?

Visual appearance may change if images are compressed, and modifying a digitally signed PDF can invalidate its digital signature. Keep the original signed document and check signature validity after any processing.

Can I compress a PDF to exactly 200 KB?

An exact output size cannot be guaranteed for every file. The result depends on page count, image content and the available compression method. Always check the final size against the receiving website's rules.
