How to Reduce PDF File Size for Email and Online Uploads

A large PDF can be difficult to email, upload to an application form or share over a slow connection. Reducing its file size can make it easier to send, but the document should remain readable and complete.

Open the free PDF Compressor

1. Check what is making the PDF large

Scanned pages and photographs often account for much of a PDF's size. A document containing mostly selectable text may already be relatively small. Check the file size and open the PDF before changing it.

2. Compress the PDF

  1. Open the QuickTools24 PDF Compressor.
  2. Select the PDF from your device.
  3. Choose an available compression setting that suits your needs.
  4. Start processing and wait for the compressed file.
  5. Download the result and compare its size with the original.

Compress a PDF now

3. Optimize scanned pages and images

If you created the PDF from a scanner or phone camera, use a suitable scan resolution and avoid unnecessarily large images. For documents that only need to be read on screen, extremely high-resolution photographs may not be necessary. Keep a higher-quality original when print quality or archival detail matters.

4. Remove content you do not need

Delete blank or duplicate pages and remove unnecessary attachments where appropriate. If only a few pages are required, use a PDF splitting or page-extraction tool rather than sending the entire document.

5. Check the result before sending

  • Open the compressed PDF and check every page.
  • Zoom in to confirm small text, signatures and numbers remain readable.
  • Check page order, orientation, tables and images.
  • Confirm the final file meets the recipient's upload-size limit.
  • Keep the original file until the recipient has accepted the upload.

Does a smaller PDF always mean lower quality?

No. Some optimization methods remove redundant data without visibly changing a document. Other methods reduce image resolution or quality to achieve a smaller file. The best setting depends on the PDF's contents and how it will be used. Always inspect the output rather than assuming the smallest file is best.

Frequently asked questions

How can I reduce a PDF for email?

Try a PDF compressor, remove pages you do not need and check whether large scanned images can be optimized. Follow the attachment limit set by your email provider.

Can I compress a PDF on a phone?

Yes. Open the QuickTools24 PDF Compressor in a current mobile browser, select your document and download the processed file. Large documents may take longer on some phones.

Will compressing a PDF remove its text?

Compression should not automatically remove text, but results depend on the method and the document. Check text selection, search, links and signatures after processing.

Is the smallest possible PDF always best?

No. A file that is too heavily compressed may make text or scanned details difficult to read. Choose a balance between file size and readability.

Final checklist

Before emailing or uploading, make sure the PDF opens correctly, contains all required pages, remains readable and meets the stated file-size limit. Keep a copy of the original until the task is complete.
