How to Crop PDF Pages Online

Open Crop PDF tool

PDF pages sometimes contain wide white borders, scanner edges or extra space that makes a document harder to read on a phone or print neatly. Cropping lets you change the visible page area so the important content is easier to focus on.

This guide explains how to crop PDF pages online, how to avoid cutting off important information and what to check before sharing the edited file.

Ready to try it? Open the QuickTools24 Crop PDF Pages tool to select a document and adjust its crop area in the browser.

How to crop a PDF with QuickTools24

  1. Open the Crop PDF Pages tool. Visit the Crop PDF Pages workspace from your browser.
  2. Select your PDF. Choose the document from your phone, tablet or computer and wait for its pages to load.
  3. Choose the page to adjust. Review the page preview and identify the extra margins or edges you want to remove.
  4. Set the crop area. Use the on-screen crop selection. On a phone, drag with your finger; on a computer, use the mouse. Keep the selection inside the page and include every line, page number, stamp or signature that must remain visible.
  5. Apply the crop. Review the selected area before processing. If the tool offers page-specific or apply-to-pages choices, select only the pages you intend to change.
  6. Download and inspect the result. Open the new PDF and check several pages, especially pages with content close to the edges.

Why crop a PDF?

Remove extra white margins

Scanned documents may have uneven borders or large blank areas. Cropping can make the actual document content occupy more of the page preview. Do not remove margins that contain notes, stamps, signatures or other required details.

Trim scanner edges

A scan may include a dark line, shadow or part of the scanner lid around the page. Adjust the crop boundary to exclude the unwanted edge while keeping the full document readable.

Prepare pages for easier viewing

When a page contains a small area of relevant content, removing unnecessary space can make that content easier to focus on. Cropping does not rewrite or summarize the text; it changes the visible page boundary.

Before you crop: check these details

  • Keep page numbers: check the bottom and top edges before trimming.
  • Protect signatures and stamps: official documents may have important marks near the margins.
  • Check every page: pages in the same PDF may use different layouts, so one crop boundary may not suit all pages.
  • Keep an original copy: save the untouched PDF until you have checked the cropped version.
  • Confirm print requirements: forms and applications may require a particular page size or margin. Follow the receiving office's instructions.

Does cropping make a PDF smaller?

Not necessarily. Cropping changes the visible page area, but the original embedded images or other data may remain in the file. If your main goal is a smaller attachment, use the QuickTools24 PDF Compressor or PDF size reducer guide. Always inspect text and image clarity after compression.

Can I crop a PDF on Android or iPhone?

Yes. Open the crop tool in an up-to-date mobile browser, select the PDF through your device's file picker and adjust the crop selection with touch controls. Very large or image-heavy documents can use substantial memory, so close unused browser tabs if processing is slow.

Can I crop only one page?

If the tool provides page-specific controls, select the page you want to change and leave the other pages untouched. Before downloading, verify that the output has the intended page count and that the remaining pages still look correct.

What is the difference between cropping and splitting a PDF?

Cropping changes the visible area of a page. Splitting separates a document into multiple PDF files or extracts a page range. If you need separate files rather than trimmed page edges, use the PDF Splitter and read the guide to splitting a PDF online.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove white borders from every page?

You can crop page margins, but inspect each page carefully. Different pages may need different crop boundaries, and important content can be close to the edge.

Will cropping reduce PDF quality?

Cropping is not the same as image compression. Still, check the output at normal zoom to make sure text, diagrams and signatures remain clear.

Can I undo a crop?

Keep the original PDF before editing. If the crop removes something important, return to the original and create a new cropped copy with a wider selection.

Is it safe to crop an official document online?

Use a service only when its privacy practices suit your document. Avoid uploading sensitive records to unfamiliar websites, and keep a secure original copy. QuickTools24's tools are designed for browser-based processing; review the site's Privacy Policy for details about website data.
