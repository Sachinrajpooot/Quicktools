When should you convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is a lossless image format that is often useful for screenshots, interface graphics, diagrams and images that will be edited again. Converting a JPG file to PNG creates a PNG copy, but it cannot restore fine details that were already lost when the JPG was originally compressed.

Will the new PNG look sharper?

Changing the file format does not add missing detail. The output can look similar to the source JPG, and the PNG file may be larger because PNG compression works differently. Keep the original JPG so you can compare both files and choose the one that suits your task.

Does JPG to PNG add transparency?

No. Converting a normal JPG to PNG does not automatically remove its background or make it transparent. Use a dedicated background-removal tool if that is what you need.

Check the output

After conversion, open the PNG and inspect small text, edges and colours. If a website requires JPG or limits upload size, keep a JPG version instead. You can use the PNG to JPG converter or Image Compressor for related tasks.
